About me

I’m a visual designer with a background in professional communications who designs brand systems and digital experiences.

My path into design started in film. I earned my BFA in Film and Television Production from the University of Southern California (USC) School of Cinematic Arts in 2023, where I studied production design and cinematography. My studies led me to the Production Designers Collective (PDC), an international community of film and television production designers, where I create visual content and digital communications across social media, graphic design and editorial.

Since 2025, I’ve also taken on freelance branding and web design projects. Most recently, I designed the website for the 2026 Production Designers Gathering, the PDC’s biennial conference in Greece.

Alongside my work, I’m pursuing an M.S. in Graphic Information Technology at Arizona State University’s Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering, where I’m building my skills in user interface and product design.

I’m especially interested in how design can make experiences more accessible and inclusive. I’ve volunteered with the USC Keck School of Medicine’s Bionic Ear Lab, supporting its Cochlear Implant (CI) Music Hour program, and this spring, my independent study at ASU will explore cinema captioning systems for Deaf and hard-of-hearing moviegoers.

When I’m not working on design projects, I love taking photos of the moon.
