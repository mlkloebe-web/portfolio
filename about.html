About me

I'm a visual designer who works across graphic design, branding and digital experiences.

I earned my BFA in Film and Television Production from the University of Southern California (USC) School of Cinematic Arts, where I studied production design and cinematography. After graduating from USC, I began working with the Production Designers Collective (PDC), an international community of film and television production designers, first as an intern in 2023 and later returning as Communications Coordinator.

In my current role at the PDC, I create visual content and digital communications across social media, graphic design and editorial content. Since 2025, my freelance design work has also grown into branding and web design. Most recently, I designed the website for the 2026 Production Designers Gathering, the PDC’s biennial conference in Greece.

As my work has expanded, so has my interest in how design shapes digital experiences and connects people. Now, I am pursuing an MS in Graphic Information Technology at Arizona State University's Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering, where I'm expanding my skills in user interface and product design.

Alongside my professional work, I'm particularly interested in how design can make user experiences more accessible and inclusive. I've previously volunteered with the USC Keck School of Medicine's Bionic Ear Lab, where I supported its Cochlear Implant (CI) Music Hour program, and in Spring 2027 at ASU, I'm exploring cinema captioning systems for Deaf and hard-of-hearing moviegoers in the United States as part of my graduate independent study.

When I'm not working on design projects, I love taking photos of the moon.
