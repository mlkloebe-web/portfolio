Featured Work

Cassie Howell

  • Brand Identity
  • Logo Design

Cassandra Claire Howell, who goes by Cassie, is a writer-director and photographer who needed a brand system for her website and the business graphics that market her services. I designed a film-inspired logo system, color palette and type system, and documented them in a logo user guide so the identity could be applied consistently.

Role
Brand Designer
Deliverables
Logo System · Color Palette · Type System · Logo User Guide
Tools
Adobe Illustrator · Figma
Year
2026

Logo System

The mark

The primary logo is a combination mark: Cassie’s CCH monogram set between forms that represent film sprocket perforations and exposure times.

Cassie shoots on analog film, so the mark draws directly from her medium: the perforations along the edge of a film strip and the exposures captured on it.

Variations

I built the mark into a system of variations, so the identity works at different sizes and in different contexts:

  • Badge logos that set the primary logo in light or dark brand colors, keeping it accessible across different backgrounds
  • A simplified CCH lettermark for contexts where the full combination logo can’t be used
  • Combination marks that pair the monogram with the full name, plus a nickname version (Cassie Claire Howell) for more informal or space-sensitive uses
  • Full-name and nickname logotypes in horizontal and vertical lockups
  • A condensed “CH” favicon for social and web icons

Color and Type

Color

Black and white neutrals form the primary palette for interfaces and layouts. Seven vivid accents, from butter and orange to green and blue, are used sparingly to add emphasis and visual interest.

Typography

Helvetica is the primary typeface, chosen for a boldness that feels clear and timeless on web interfaces. I chose Jura as the secondary typeface because its monospaced roots recall typewriters, another medium Cassie works in, while staying clean enough for digital use. It also adds a subtle edge that contrasts with Helvetica’s weight and neutrality.

  • Jura Bold for display text and all-caps bylines
  • Helvetica Bold for titles and subtitles
  • Helvetica Regular for body text and captions

Logo user guide

I documented the system in a logo user guide, a framework for how to implement the brand’s logos, typefaces and colors.

Reflection

This project shows my approach to identity design at an individual-client scale. Every part of the system started from the tools Cassie actually uses: analog film shaped the mark, and the typewriter shaped the type. Building the mark into a system of variations then let that idea carry from the full logo all the way down to a favicon.
