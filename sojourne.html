The Concept

The hotel

Sojourne is a fictional boutique hotel in New York, imagined as an old-world hotel for modern New York. The brand needed to feel rich and historic, with wood-paneled rooms, candlelit dining and classic cocktails, while still speaking to a contemporary city audience.

[The name: why “Sojourne,” and why the spelling with an e]

Identity

Logo

The identity pairs a serif wordmark with an SJ monogram topped by a crest, used on its own or alongside the wordmark, and in light and dark versions.

[The idea behind the SJ monogram and crest, once the Sojourne version is final]

Color and type

The palette centers on a deep oxblood red, balanced by pink, cream and black.

[Your typefaces, and why you chose the palette and type]

Instagram campaign

I designed a pre-opening Instagram campaign to build anticipation before reservations open. The profile sets up story highlights for Rooms, Dining, NYC, Events and Press, and the grid alternates moody interior photography with typographic posts.

Each typographic post gives followers a reason to sign up early:

  • “Reservations Coming Soon” announces the opening
  • “Be the First at the Table” invites followers to join a mailing list for restaurant updates
  • “Your Room Awaits” offers first access to reservations
